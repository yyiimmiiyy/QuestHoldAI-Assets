## 9. Free play, the trial and Pro

- **Free:** up to 100 turns a day. A turn is one action you take that the Game Master answers; menus, maps, dice rolls, Ask the GM, recaps and the Game Master's own retries do not count, and an action you take back with Undo is given back. If you reach the limit partway through a scene, you get up to 5 more turns to finish it (up to 10 during a fight). The count runs over a rolling 24 hours and is kept on your phone. Free play continues one world of your own at a time; Dust Hollow and the sample worlds are always free. Your other worlds stay saved on your phone and you can read them, but only Pro continues them. Nothing is deleted.
- **Trial:** for the first 7 days after you install QuestHold, everything in Pro is unlocked.
- **Pro:** a one-time purchase of $19.99 USD (the price can differ by country; Google Play shows you the price before you buy). It is not a subscription. Pro removes the daily turn limit and unlocks the Pro features listed in the app.
- **Payment:** purchases go through Google Play. We never see or store your card details.
- **Refunds:** refunds follow Google Play's refund policies; ask Google Play for one. A refunded purchase ends Pro.
- **Restoring Pro:** Pro belongs to the Google account you bought it with. Install QuestHold on a phone signed in to that account to get it back.
